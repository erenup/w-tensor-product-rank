\section{Coordinate factors in the Gram entries}\label{app:entries}

This appendix records the cancellation and degree checks used in
\Cref{lem:blocks}. For a basic function indexed by $\jmath$ and a
function from $\cB_i$ indexed by $(s,\iota)$, their product with
$H_t$ is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cross-basic}
P_{ab}(Z)=
Z_i^{s+\jmath_i}h_i(Z_i)^{d_i-s-1}
\prod_{j\ne i}Z_j^{\iota_j+\jmath_j}h_j(Z_j)^{d_j-2}.
\end{equation}
The exponent $d_i-s-1$ is nonnegative because $s\le d_i-1$.
The degrees in coordinate $i$ range from $s+\jmath_i\ge2$ to
$d_i-1+\jmath_i\le d_i$. Thus the $i$th factor has zero
$L_i$-value. Each other factor has degree at most $d_j$.
Applying \eqref{eq:product} gives $L(P_{ab})=0$.

Next choose functions from $\cB_i$ and $\cB_j$, with $i\ne j$.
Let their indices be $(s,\iota)$ and $(s',\jmath)$, respectively.
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cross-additional}
\begin{split}
P_{ab}(Z)={}&
Z_i^{s+\jmath_i}h_i(Z_i)^{d_i-s-1}
Z_j^{s'+\iota_j}h_j(Z_j)^{d_j-s'-1}\\
&\times\prod_{m\ne i,j}
Z_m^{\iota_m+\jmath_m}h_m(Z_m)^{d_m-2}.
\end{split}
\end{equation}
Both distinguished coordinate factors have nonnegative powers of
$h_i,h_j$ and monomial degrees between $2$ and their respective
degree bounds. All remaining factors are also within their bounds.
The product rule therefore gives $L(P_{ab})=0$.

Finally, for two functions from the same family $\cB_i$,
the coordinate factors are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:same-family}
P_{ab}(Z)=Z_i^{s+s'}h_i(Z_i)^{d_i-s-s'}
\prod_{j\ne i}Z_j^{\iota_j+\jmath_j}h_j(Z_j)^{d_j-2}.
\end{equation}
This is polynomial when $s+s'\le d_i$, yielding the zero blocks
in \Cref{lem:anti}. At $s+s'=d_i+1$, one denominator remains,
yielding $H_i$ in \eqref{eq:Hi}. In particular, this last case
is not an application of the polynomial moment formula to a
rational function; it requires the calculation below.
