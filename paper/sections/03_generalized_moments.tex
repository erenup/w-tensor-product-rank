\section{Generalized moment conditions}\label{sec:generalized}

Let $\cP_{\bd}:=\{P\in\C[Z_1,\ldots,Z_k]:\deg_{Z_i}P\le d_i
\text{ for all }i\}$. Define the weighted evaluation functional
\begin{equation}\label{eq:L}
L(P):=\sum_{\ell=1}^r w_\ell P(z_\ell),
\qquad P\in\cP_{\bd}.
\end{equation}
For each $i$, let $L_i$ be the linear functional on univariate
polynomials of degree at most $d_i$ determined by $L_i(Z_i^e)=m_i(e)$.

\begin{lemma}[Generalized moment conditions]\label{lem:generalized}
The moment conditions \eqref{eq:moments} are equivalent to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:generalized}
L(P)=\sum_e p_e\prod_{i=1}^k m_i(e_i)
\quad\text{for every }P(Z)=\sum_e p_e\prod_{i=1}^k Z_i^{e_i}
\in\cP_{\bd}.
\end{equation}
In particular, for $P(Z)=\prod_iP_i(Z_i)$ with $\deg P_i\le d_i$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:product}
L\!\left(\prod_{i=1}^kP_i(Z_i)\right)=\prod_{i=1}^kL_i(P_i).
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Expand $P$ in the monomial basis and apply \eqref{eq:moments} term by
term. Conversely, taking $P(Z)=\prod_iZ_i^{e_i}$ in
\eqref{eq:generalized} recovers each moment condition. Expanding the
univariate factors gives \eqref{eq:product}.
\end{proof}

Thus the normalized decomposition, the monomial moment conditions,
and the generalized moment conditions are equivalent descriptions
of the same identity. Formula \eqref{eq:generalized} determines $L(P)$
from the target polynomial whenever $P$ lies in the specified degree
range. We also use $L(R)=\sum_\ell w_\ell R(z_\ell)$ for rational
functions $R$ defined at every point, but \eqref{eq:generalized} does
not directly apply to such functions.
