\begin{abstract}
We study the minimum number of products of powers of binary linear forms
needed to express $\prod_{i=1}^k x_i^{d_i-1}y_i$.
For $k\ge2$ and $d_i\ge3$, we prove that this number is
$2^{k-1}(\sum_i d_i-2k+2)$, matching the upper bound of
Canino, Casarotti and Santarsiero. The lower bound starts from an arbitrary
complex decomposition. A change of coordinates converts its coefficients
into weighted points satisfying product moment conditions. We construct
rational test functions whose evaluation vectors have a block diagonal
weighted Gram matrix. Its blocks are nondegenerate at one finite
choice of auxiliary parameters, so the number of decomposition terms is
at least the number of test functions. The argument allows repeated points
and zero coordinates and uses no positivity assumption.
We also determine the exact partially symmetric rank of every product of
binary monomials $x_i^{a_i}y_i^{b_i}$ with $a_i\ge b_i\ge1$, with a
matching explicit decomposition.
\end{abstract}
