# Review record

These reports record the independent informal AI-agent audits of the
paper's mathematics. Each auditor worked separately from the others and from
the authors' text where stated, derived the statements first, checked every
step, and ran its own exact tests. No report claims formal certification or
external peer review.

to the version it reviewed. Reports 01–02 reviewed Sections 1–7 in the
manuscript of 1 November 2026, and the binary-monomial theorem in an earlier
write-up (files `lower_bound.tex`, `upper_bound.tex`, `context.tex`) together
